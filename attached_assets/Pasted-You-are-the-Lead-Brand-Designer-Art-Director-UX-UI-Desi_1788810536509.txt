You are the Lead Brand Designer, Art Director, UX/UI Designer, and Product Design Strategist for a new EdTech platform.

Before writing or changing any application code, I want you to develop a complete, professional brand identity for this product.

## PRODUCT CONTEXT

The product is a modern online live-class platform inside a larger platform called "جاهز".

The service is essentially an online education center where independent teachers can create and host live classes, and students can discover, purchase, and attend those classes.

Teachers can:

* Create live classes
* Schedule classes in advance
* Publish "Premiere" upcoming classes
* Upload a class thumbnail
* Set the class title, description, subject, grade, date/time, and price
* Build a following
* Receive ratings
* Track students, attendance, classes taught, and earnings

Students can:

* Discover live classes
* See who is live now
* Browse upcoming/premiere classes
* Follow teachers
* Receive notifications
* Purchase a class
* Join the live class
* Watch replays
* Rate teachers
* Track their learning history

The product should feel like:

EDUCATION + LIVE CLASSROOM + TEACHER COMMUNITY + DISCOVERY + ENERGY

It should NOT feel like:

* A boring traditional school
* A corporate LMS
* A generic SaaS dashboard
* A childish kids' education app
* A cheap tutoring marketplace

The brand should feel:

* Modern
* Smart
* Energetic
* Friendly
* Creative
* Educational
* Trustworthy
* Human
* Youthful without being childish
* Premium enough to compete with serious EdTech products

The core visual inspiration should come from:

* Whiteboards
* Chalkboards
* Handwritten notes
* Marker strokes
* Doodles
* Underlines
* Circles around important points
* Arrows
* Highlighting
* Diagrams
* Mathematical symbols
* Teacher handwriting
* Classroom energy
* The moment when a teacher explains something and suddenly "it clicks"

But DO NOT simply make the design look like a literal school board.

The goal is to abstract these ideas into a sophisticated modern brand system.

# PHASE 1 — BRAND STRATEGY

First, propose 3 distinct brand directions.

For each direction provide:

1. Brand concept
2. Emotional personality
3. Visual philosophy
4. Logo concept
5. Color philosophy
6. Typography direction
7. Illustration style
8. Iconography style
9. UI visual language
10. Why it fits this product
11. What makes it different from generic EdTech brands

The three directions should be genuinely different, not minor variations.

For example:

Direction A:
"Modern Whiteboard"

Direction B:
"Teacher's Notebook"

Direction C:
"Live Classroom Energy"

These are examples only. Create better concepts yourself.

Then choose the strongest direction and explain why.

---

# PHASE 2 — BRAND NAME

The product name may not be finalized yet.

Generate 15–25 potential brand names.

The names should ideally work in Arabic, English, or both.

Explore concepts related to:

* Class
* Lesson
* Knowledge
* Board
* Learning
* Teacher
* Live
* Notes
* Explanation
* Progress
* Community

Avoid generic names that sound like existing education startups.

For every serious candidate provide:

* Name
* Arabic rendering if applicable
* Meaning/concept
* Brand personality
* Why it works
* How it could look as a logo
* Whether it sounds premium, youthful, academic, or playful

Then shortlist the strongest 5.

DO NOT assume the final name without presenting the reasoning first.

If the existing product architecture already contains a temporary service name, treat it as provisional until the brand direction is established.
